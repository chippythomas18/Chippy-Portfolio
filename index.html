About

Engineering with context, care and ownership.

I'm Chippy, a software engineer who is just as comfortable with the business side, working out what to build as well as building it.

Over five years I've worked across requirements, architecture, frontend, backend, databases, testing, cloud deployments and production support, for agencies, startups and product teams. On Kolekt I was also the client's business analyst: running requirements conversations with the founder, writing user stories, estimating work and keeping everyone aligned through every release.

Most of my production work is in PHP/Laravel, Vue.js, MySQL and AWS, but I'm a fundamentals-first engineer who picks up a team's stack quickly. I'm open to software developer and business analyst roles, especially where the two meet. I'm based in Brampton, Ontario.
