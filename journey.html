2021 — 2024
Bridge Global Software Solutions

Software Engineer

Dec 2021 – Jun 2024 · Product engineering for international clients

At Bridge Global I moved from building websites to engineering products that people depend on every day. On Kolekt I was the only full-stack developer on a live platform with 1,000+ users. I learned APIs, Lumen and Vue.js on the job, then rebuilt its transaction and points system.

What I built

  • Designed and maintained REST APIs in Laravel and Lumen, including APIs that improved mobile app functionality and performance.
  • Built Vue.js and Nuxt.js frontends, including dashboards and client portals.
  • Implemented Laravel events, listeners, queues and background jobs for asynchronous workflows.
  • Added API authentication and authorization for secure access.

How I worked

  • Optimized MySQL and MongoDB data storage for performance and scalability.
  • Wrote PHPUnit tests to improve quality and reduce regressions.
  • Used AWS S3 for storage and Docker for containerized deployments.
  • Diagnosed and fixed production issues through debugging, monitoring and hotfixes.
  • Owned work from requirements through deployment and maintenance, with consistent on-time delivery.
  • Worked with clients and cross-functional teams on requirements analysis, user stories, technical documentation and task estimation, and on Kolekt took the business analyst role for the founder.
What it taught me: how to design for change (API versioning, backward compatibility), how to own production, and that I enjoy the business analysis side as much as the code. Understanding the problem first makes the software better.
